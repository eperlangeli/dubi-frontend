## Data Safety Working Answers

Current app behavior to disclose:

- Account login/authentication data is collected.
- Profile and body data entered by the user is collected.
- Nutrition preferences, meal data, goals, progress entries, and app settings are collected.
- Data is sent to DUBI backend/Supabase infrastructure to provide account sync and app functionality.
- Data is not sold.
- Data is not shared with advertisers in the current beta.
- Account deletion is available in-app from Settings.
- Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Likely Play Console data types:

- Personal info: email address, user IDs, and name if collected during onboarding/account setup.
- Health and fitness: nutrition, body metrics, goals, progress, activity or wearable-derived signals when enabled.
- App activity: app interactions needed to operate the service, if logged by the backend.
- Device or other IDs: only if analytics/crash tooling is later added.

Current release note:

```text
The current Android beta does not request native Health Connect permissions. Wearable and health integrations are planned for a later phase and must be reflected in Data safety before release.
```

## Health Apps Declaration

DUBI should be treated as a health/wellness app because it handles nutrition, body, progress, and future wearable data.

Suggested declaration framing:

```text
DUBI is a wellness and nutrition planning app. It helps users organize nutrition goals, meals, progress, and lifestyle signals. DUBI is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or clinical decision-making.
```

For the current Android beta:

- Do not request Health Connect permissions yet.
- Do disclose that the app handles user-entered nutrition/wellness data.
- Before adding Health Connect, prepare exact data-type justifications for each requested permission.

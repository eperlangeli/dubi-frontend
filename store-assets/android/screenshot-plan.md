# Android Screenshot Plan

Capture after final beta UI is installed from the signed build.

Recommended 6 screenshots:

1. Welcome/onboarding start.
2. Profile setup with goals/preferences.
3. Today/dashboard nutrition plan.
4. Meal detail or meal planning flow.
5. Progress/trends.
6. Settings with privacy/account controls.

Rules:

- Use real app UI, not mockups.
- Do not include personal email, phone, address or private health data.
- Do not show before/after body images.
- Do not make medical or guaranteed outcome claims.
- Export PNG/JPEG, max 8 MB each.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026. This page describes how DUBI handles data for the current beta app and web service.

Data collected

DUBI collects the information you provide to create and use your account, including email address, profile details, nutrition goals, body metrics, preferences, meal choices, progress entries, app settings and consent choices.

How data is used

Your data is used to provide account access, generate and adapt your nutrition plan, sync your information across devices, support safety and privacy controls, and improve the reliability of the beta service.

Wearables and health data

The current beta may ask about lifestyle and wellness signals entered by the user. Native Health Connect, HealthKit and wearable-provider integrations are planned for later phases. If enabled in the future, DUBI will request explicit consent and use only the data needed for app functionality.

Sharing

DUBI does not sell your data. During the beta, DUBI does not share your data with advertisers, insurers or data brokers. Data is processed by DUBI infrastructure and service providers needed to operate the app.

Security

DUBI uses HTTPS for data transmission. Native app session tokens are stored using platform storage through Capacitor Preferences where supported. Android automatic app backup is disabled for the native app.

Delete your data

You can permanently delete your DUBI account and saved data from Settings using “Delete my account and data”.

Beta notice

DUBI is a wellness and nutrition planning beta. It is not a medical diagnosis service and does not replace professional medical advice.

Contact

For privacy or support requests, contact: support@dubi.health.
